## Brand & Style

The design system embodies warm modernism and refined literary elegance, balancing the quiet atmosphere of a physical artist’s folio with the living fluidity of an intellectual digital garden. It caters to discerning design directors, curators, design engineers, and creative collaborators who value contemplative depth, typographically driven storytelling, and precise interaction craft.

The visual style merges:
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Generous negative space, delicate hair-thin dividers, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and intentional compositional pacing inspired by independent print periodicals.
- **Physical Paper Tactility:** Surfaces evoke matte archival stock, unbleached alabaster sheets, and soft cast shadows rather than cold digital canvases.
- **Atmospheric Softness:** Gentle diffused ambient glows, subtle glassmorphic accents, and fluid transitions that reward patient reading without visual noise or terminal/hacker tropes.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in soft, warm paper tones complemented by deep graphite ink and muted Morandi-inspired vegetal and earth pigments.

- **Primary (`#1E2022` - Graphite Ink):** High-contrast, warm carbon for primary headers, titles, high-priority interactive states, and grounded anchors.
- **Secondary (`#6E8879` - Muted Sage):** A quiet, vegetal Morandi green used for digital garden status badges ("growing", "tended"), active category filters, and contemplative accent highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#B07D62` - Warm Clay):** A soft terracotta hue for deliberate focal points, delicate hover callouts, and curated date tags.
- **Accent Fog (`#7E97A6` - Misty Ice):** An ethereal secondary accent for informational metadata, fluid tags, and subtle visual tinting.
- **Base Background (`#FBFBF9` - Archival Alabaster):** The main paper surface, offering warmth that reduces glare and eliminates clinical coldness.
- **Layered Surfaces:**
  - Surface elevated: `#FFFFFF` with 70% opacity and gentle backdrop blur.
  - Surface muted: `#F5F5F0` (warm pebble paper tone for nested sections and code/quote backdrops).
  - Subtle borders: `#E6E6DF` (1px warm stone line weight).
  - Subtle text: `#636B74` (slate pebble for long-form narrative body and subtext).

## Typography

The typographic expression centers on an intentional counterpoint between **Newsreader** (an authoritative, lyrical serif with literary cut and optical grace) and **Hanken Grotesk** (a contemporary, precise grotesque that recedes cleanly to facilitate sustained reading).

- **Editorial Serifs:** Reserved for headlines, article titles, poetic intro quotes, and reflective essays. Italic variations in Newsreader should be embraced for subtle cadence, foreign terms, publication names, and introspective project notes.
- **Modern Grotesque Body:** Set at relaxed line heights (1.6 to 1.7x) to emulate long-form monograph print layouts.
- **Curatorial Meta & Badges:** `label-caps` is rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`+0.08em`) to denote project statuses, dates, metadata categories, and taxonomy markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric, generous editorial grid designed to feel spacious, paced, and unhurried:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column variable grid on desktop with a maximum canvas width of `88rem` (`1408px`). Long-form essays and garden notes default to a single constrained column of `44rem` (`704px`) centered or offset by a 3-column metadata margin.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** Vertical sections breathe through expanded increments (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`). Project showcases use deliberate off-center margins to mimic bespoke gallery catalogs rather than dense SaaS dashboards.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Asymmetric side margins, floating dynamic header pills, two-to-three column showcase galleries.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Two-column reflow, sidebars collapse beneath main thesis, horizontal padding set to `2rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column flow, gutter pinned to `1.25rem`, headers scale gracefully to designated mobile variants to maintain seamless line wrapping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh synthetic drop shadows or high drop z-indexes. Depth is expressed through translucent tactile strata:

- **Low-Contrast Architectural Hairlines:** Dividers and card frames use `1px solid #E6E6DF` (warm stone) to establish crisp, quiet physical boundaries.
- **Ambient Paper Shadows:** When lifted, elements cast warm, highly diffused ambient silhouettes:
  - Default card rest: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.02), 0 8px 24px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.03)`
  - Elevated / Active hover: `0 12px 32px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.05), 0 2px 6px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.02)`
- **Spotlight Overlays & Frosted Glass:** Translucent pill elements and floating navigation surfaces utilize `rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%)` and an internal rim highlight of `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
- **Soft Ambient Glows:** Subtle radial gradients of sage (`#6E8879`) and terracotta (`#B07D62`) at 4%–6% opacity act as diffuse background illumination zones under featured case study media.

## Shapes

The primary shape philosophy is architectural, quiet, and subtle. 

- **Containers & Project Cards:** Governed by `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` up to `0.5rem` / `8px` for larger cards), mimicking the clean cut of trimmed paper plates and book boards.
- **Interactive Utility Pills:** Tags, garden status indicators, and floating menu navigations adopt full pill capsules to provide a sensory, pebble-like contrast against the structured architectural grid.
- **Visual Media:** Media embeds, mockups, and canvases inherit uniform `6px` border radii accompanied by internal inset hair-thin borders to simulate framed print specimens.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Button:** Deep graphite ink (`#1E2022`) background with off-white text (`#FBFBF9`). Straightforward `4px` radius, padded at `0.65rem 1.25rem`. On hover, softens slightly into `#2D3139` with a subtle `-1px` vertical translation.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link:** Newsreader italic or tracked grotesque label with a continuous hair-thin underline positioned `4px` below baseline (`text-underline-offset: 4px`), shifting to terracotta (`#B07D62`) or sage (`#6E8879`) on hover with smooth 200ms ease.
- **Glass Capsule Button:** Pill-shaped, translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), 1px border (`#E6E6DF`), gentle hover scale (`1.015`).

### Chips & Garden Taxonomy Tags
- **Seedling / Growing Status Badges:** Enclosed pill tags featuring a tiny `6px` solid dot indicator.
  - "Sprout / Idea": dot colored with muted terracotta (`#B07D62`), background tint `rgba(176, 125, 98, 0.08)`.
  - "Growing / In Progress": dot colored with muted sage (`#6E8879`), background tint `rgba(110, 136, 121, 0.08)`.
  - "Evergreen / Formed": dot colored with slate pebble (`#636B74`), background tint `rgba(99, 107, 116, 0.08)`.
- **Type:** Label caps (`11px`), tracking `+0.08em`, monochrome text.

### Cards & Project Folios
- **Composition:** Framed by 1px `#E6E6DF` borders on an unbleached `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCFA` plane. 
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Subtle `2D` or `3D` tilt transition (`transform: translateY(-3px)`) paired with the ambient shadow expansion. Images within cards feature a gentle desaturation by default (5%), returning to rich neutral vibrancy upon focus.

### Lists & Digital Garden Index
- **Index Row:** Border-bottom `1px solid #E6E6DF` with generous vertical padding (`1.25rem`). Arranged with date/type in small caps (`#636B74`), entry title in prominent serif, and growth stage tag on the right margin.
- **Hover Effect:** Row background shifts to translucent `#F5F5F0` with a smooth 150ms crossfade.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Inquiries:** Minimalist single-line or soft container fields. Background `#F5F5F0`, no heavy outer focus rings. On focus, transitions cleanly to `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp `1px solid #1E2022` border. Placeholder text in `#8C939B` italicized or understated sans.

### Curatorial Footnotes & Aside Blocks
- **Margin Notes (Sidenotes):** Pinned adjacent to body narrative on desktop, formatted in `body-sm` (`13px`) with slate ink color (`#636B74`) and a delicate `2px` left border in Morandi sage.
